Other business

01

OPTIMUS

OPTIMUS COMMODITIES LLC is established under the SHAMS regulator (Sharjah UAE). It is a leading financial company which trades in the various arbitrage opportunities that exists across various Global Exchanges. The principal counters are the various currency futures pair (USD INR, USD EUR, USD TWD, USD IDR etc), FX-OTC products, FX and Equity options, Non-Deliverable Forwards etc. On the Commodities and precious metals side we do a substantial volume of trading in DGCX (Precious metals, Energy products and Agri products) and LME (All base metals)

02

VINTAGE

VINTAGE CRYPTO FZCO is being established to capitalize on the surging digital currency market, focusing on trading various crypto assets on global exchanges. The company aims to leverage advanced analytical and trading software to enhance its technological edge and refine its trading strategies. Our trading focus will include various crypto currencies listed on global platforms, primarily Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, and Binance Coin. We intend to establish trading accounts with top crypto trading providers such as Binance, Coinbase, Bitstamp, OKEx, and Bitfinex.
